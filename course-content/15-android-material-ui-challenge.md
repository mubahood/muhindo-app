# Course 15 ⭐ — Android Material UI Design Challenge

**Tier 2 · Frameworks & Specialisation · Level: Intermediate · Prerequisites: Course 14 · TOP FEATURED**

A design bootcamp for Android: each session takes one real interface and rebuilds it
with Material Design. You train your eye and your XML at the same time. Between video
sessions, short text lessons teach the Material rules the screens are silently
following.

**What you will learn**

- Material Design principles: elevation, color roles, spacing, typography
- Build polished screens: forms, login, dashboards, settings
- Turn any design screenshot into working Android XML

## Final challenge

**Seven screens in seven days.** Pick any app you admire; each day rebuild one of its
screens in Material XML (feed, detail, form, login, settings, dashboard, profile).
Post each day's screenshot; the streak is the assignment.

**Quiz ideas:** screenshot → component naming · spacing-grid spot-the-error ·
color-role matching (primary vs surface vs error) · streak project graded on the 7 screens.

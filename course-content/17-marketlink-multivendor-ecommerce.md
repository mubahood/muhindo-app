# Course 17 ⭐ — MarketLink: Multi-Vendor E-Commerce Platform (Laravel + Flutter)

**Tier 3 · Capstone Systems · Level: Advanced · Prerequisites: Courses 10–13 (Course 16 recommended first) · TOP FEATURED**

Build a real marketplace — many sellers, many products, one platform (think Jumia or
Jiji). The back office runs on Laravel; the shopping experience is a polished Flutter
app with sliders, product screens, image zoom and related products.

**System features:** vendor & product management, bulk image handling, stock,
mobile shop UI, product detail screens, image galleries with zoom, related products.

## Graduation assignment

Launch a mini-marketplace for a niche you know (campus food, boutique fashion,
farm produce): 2 vendor accounts, 20 products with images, the Flutter shop app
browsing them live, plus your extension module of choice from 21–23.

**Milestone quizzes:** image-pipeline ordering quiz · "which table does this belong
to?" schema quiz · UI screenshot → widget quiz.

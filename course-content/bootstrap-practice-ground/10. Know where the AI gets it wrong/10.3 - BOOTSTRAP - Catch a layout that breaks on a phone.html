Lesson 10.3

Catch a layout that breaks on a phone

How it works

A layout can look fine on a laptop and still overflow on a phone. Responsive classes and flexible content help it fit different widths.

When should you use this?

Test every page at narrow and wide widths, especially pages with grids, images and tables.

Key points

  • Use the device toolbar to test several widths.
  • Look for sideways scrolling.
  • Use flexible images and responsive grid classes.

In short

A layout is ready when its content fits and stays usable at small and large widths.

Try it yourself

Use the device toolbar at 360, 768 and 1280 px. Find and fix any horizontal scrolling.

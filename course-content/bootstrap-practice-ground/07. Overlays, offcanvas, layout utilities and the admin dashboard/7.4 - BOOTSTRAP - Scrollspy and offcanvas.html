Scrollspy and offcanvas

How it works

Offcanvas slides a panel in from the side. Scrollspy updates navigation based on the section currently in view.

When should you use this?

Use offcanvas for filters or navigation on a narrow screen. Use scrollspy on a long page with clear sections.

Key points

  • The offcanvas trigger target must match its id.
  • Scrollspy links need matching section ids.
  • Do not hide the only way to reach important content.

In short

These tools help with long pages and small screens when their targets are correct.

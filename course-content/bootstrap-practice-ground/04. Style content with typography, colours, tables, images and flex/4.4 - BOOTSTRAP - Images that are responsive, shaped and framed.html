Lesson 4.4

Images that are responsive, shaped and framed

How it works

The img-fluid class lets an image shrink with its parent while keeping its proportions. Bootstrap also has classes for rounded and framed images.

Example

Keep a product photo inside its column

Rice, 5 kg

The image scales with its column.

Code for this example

HTML

<div class="row align-items-center g-3">
  <div class="col-sm-5">
    <img class="img-fluid rounded shadow-sm" src="../images/products/rice-5kg.webp" alt="A bag of Marigold rice">
  </div>
  <div class="col-sm-7">
    <h3 class="h5">
      Rice, 5 kg
    </h3>
    <p class="mb-0">
      The image scales with its column.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

When should you use this?

Use img-fluid for product photos and other images that appear in flexible columns.

Key points

  • Add useful alt text.
  • Use height auto to keep the image shape.
  • Choose image shapes only when they fit the content.

In short

A responsive image stays inside its layout and remains understandable without sight.

Try it yourself

Try rounded-circle and img-thumbnail. Keep the photo responsive and its alternative text useful.

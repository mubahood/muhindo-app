Lesson 4.2.1

Advanced typography, colours, text and backgrounds

How it works

This bonus lesson walks the same ground as Lesson 4.2, but slower and with the W3Schools reference open beside it. It covers the parts the main lesson passes over: text utilities for wrapping and truncation, opacity on text and backgrounds, background gradients, and how the subtle colour variants behave.

<p class="text-truncate" style="max-width:14rem">
  This product name is far too long to fit on one line
</p>

  Subtle text on a subtle background
</p>
<p class="text-success text-opacity-50">
  Half-strength success text
</p>

When should you use this?

Take this lesson when Lesson 4.2 has landed and you want the full set. The body and subtle colour classes shown here are the ones that adapt by themselves when you add dark mode in Module 09.

Key points

  • text-truncate needs a width to work against.
  • The -emphasis and -subtle variants pair text and background at matching strength.
  • text-opacity and bg-opacity change strength without a new colour.
  • Body and subtle classes survive the switch to dark mode; bg-light and text-dark do not.

In short

The extra utilities here are the ones that keep working when the page changes theme.
